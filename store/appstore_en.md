## Description (4000)
Chess Academy teaches children and beginners chess from scratch, step by step.

20 INTERACTIVE LESSONS
From how the pieces move to checkmate, from forks and pins to opening principles: every topic is taught with short explanations and small tasks on the board. You get instant feedback when you find the right move.

700+ CHECKMATE PUZZLES
Mate-in-one and mate-in-two puzzles sorted from easy to hard. A new "puzzle of the day" every day; streaks turn learning into a habit.

5-LEVEL COMPUTER OPPONENT
Pick anything from the random-moving Rookie to the strong Grandmaster. The opponent runs on Stockfish, the world's strongest chess engine, fully offline. Play with a clock if you like.

GAME ANALYSIS
After every game your moves are reviewed one by one: mistakes, missed mates and the better move are shown on the board.

PLAY WITH A FRIEND
Two iPhones on the same Wi-Fi connect directly to each other. No account, no server, no internet needed; rooms are found by three emoji.

MAKE IT YOURS
10 board themes and 8 piece sets, light and dark mode, including the app's own "Crescent" set.

PROGRESS AND BADGES
Finished lessons, solved puzzles, daily streaks and won games earn badges.

SAFE FOR KIDS
No accounts, no ads, no data collection. Everything stays on the device and works without internet.

The first 8 lessons are free. The full set of tactics and checkmate lessons unlocks with a one-time "All Lessons" purchase; there is no subscription.

Available in English, Turkish, German and Spanish. Puzzles are selected from the lichess.org open database (CC0). The app is open source (GPL-3.0).
